## 🛡️ Reporting a Vulnerability

If you discover a security vulnerability or sandbox breakout vector, **please do not open a public issue**. Instead, report it responsibly via one of the following methods:

1. **GitHub Private Vulnerability Reporting**: Use the "Report a vulnerability" button under the **Security** tab of the relevant repository.
2. **Email**: Contact the maintainers directly at `amitpadhan525@gmail.com`.

### What to Include in Your Report
* Description of the vulnerability and its potential impact.
* Steps to reproduce, proof-of-concept (PoC) code, or crash logs.
* Affected operating systems, hardware platforms, and software versions.
* Any potential mitigations or patches you have identified.

### Disclosure Timeline
* We will acknowledge receipt of your vulnerability report within **48 hours**.
* We will provide a status update and estimated resolution schedule within **7 days**.
* Once a fix is verified, a patched release and credit attribution will be published.
